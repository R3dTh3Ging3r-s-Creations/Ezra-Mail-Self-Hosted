# Privacy policy

Ezra Mail is self-hosted software. The project owner does not operate a hosted
mail service and does not collect your mailbox data. Mailbox data is processed
by the Ezra Mail installation you run and control.

When you connect an email or calendar provider, that provider's own terms and
privacy policy apply to its services and APIs. Project interactions on GitHub
are also subject to GitHub's policies.


## Optional connected services

If you enable the personal ChatGPT plugin, requested mail, calendar and task
content is returned through your OpenAI tunnel to ChatGPT. That content is then
subject to your OpenAI account/workspace settings and policies; local storage or
local summary models do not keep plugin tool results local-only. Each installation
uses its own credentials and private connection.

Optional hosted models, Telegram and browser push also involve their configured
providers. Review the setup guide and enable only the destinations and content
you intend to share. Do not include credentials or real mailbox content in public
GitHub issues or diagnostic attachments.

Any future Ezra Cloud offering will have a separate privacy policy before it is
made available.
